    How to listen

    • Start with the six Foundations (chapters 1 to 6); every later chapter builds on them.
    • Each reading follows the same pattern: “पहले, इस लेख का सार” (what the reading says), “अब इसके पीछे की कहानी” (the background story), “याद रखने का तरीका” (a memory hook), “कुछ ज़रूरी शब्द” (key words) and “पढ़ते समय ध्यान रखें” (a reading tip).
    • This page remembers your chapter, position and speed on this device, and moves to the next chapter by itself. Lock-screen and headphone controls work on phones.
    • Open a chapter’s Contents to jump to the matching cards in the written guide (opens in a new tab, so the audio keeps playing), or to download the MP3 for offline listening.
    • Keyboard: Space = play or pause, ← = back 15 seconds, → = forward 30 seconds.
